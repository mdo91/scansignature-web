# Signature Scan editorial product facts

Verified 26 October 2026 for public version **1.1.0** against the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/signature-scan-sign-pdfs/id6805875185) and the developer's supplied product documentation/screenshots. Machine-readable constraints are in `site/lib/product-facts.json`. The hosted collector fetches current Apple metadata before new generation; a new product version must be reconciled rather than silently trusting stale claims.

Signature Scan: Sign PDFs is also called SignDocs, by Damas Technologies LLC. Its primary workflows are for iPhone/iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26 or later. It scans paper, including multiple pages, into a PDF; captures a real handwritten signature from paper; crops and removes the paper background; offers Clean, Balanced and Bold previews; and preserves supported black, red and dark-blue ink. Users can save/reuse/rename/delete signatures locally, open a PDF from Files, select a page and drag/pinch the signature, then save a separate flattened completed PDF copy. The original remains unchanged. Saved PDFs can be reopened from Recent Docs and shared with compatible destinations.

Version 1.1.0 adds supported standard PDF form fields (text, checkbox, radio and choice), preserving their positions and read-only restrictions. Flat/scanned PDFs can receive new multiline text and checkmarks; these additions do not replace, remove or recognize existing page text. Users can place multiple handwritten signatures, initials and dates, then move, resize, duplicate or delete additions and use undo/redo for session edits. Cross-page duplication requires matching page geometry; incompatible pages need manual placement. The signature camera shows the full preview with clearer framing guides. Do not invent new button names or claim independent testing.

Reusable templates have two modes: **Saved form** retains a protected local PDF copy and its layout; **Layout only** retains positions and field definitions without PDF content and needs a compatible PDF. Review the preview before applying a template. Entries, dates and handwriting are cleared for reuse; signature/initial positions require a fresh handwriting selection. Printed/flattened page content remains in Saved form templates and may contain personal information. A template is not a redaction tool, cloud synchronization service or automatic personal-information/signature profile. Template creation/editing does not consume a completed-output allowance. Completed documents are separate flattened PDF copies: do not promise that their fields remain interactive or that their flattened content can be removed later. Document processing and template storage stay on the device.

Scanning, signature extraction and PDF creation happen on device, without a processing upload or account requirement. App installation, purchases/restores, retrieving cloud-only files and external delivery have their own connection requirements. Free document exports are limited: do not invent a count or promise unlimited free use. Apple lists compatible Apple-silicon Mac availability with macOS 26 and M1 or later; this is not evidence of a separately tested native Mac experience. The listing currently identifies six interface languages: English, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and Turkish. Eleven website languages do not establish eleven app-interface languages. Supplied screenshots show English labels.

Do not claim OCR, merging existing PDF files, standalone PNG export, editing or deleting existing PDF page text, encryption, identity verification, certificate-based signatures, signature requests or legal validity. Multiple paper scans in one PDF differ from merging existing files; a background-cleaned signature differs from a separately downloadable transparent PNG. Embedded handwriting is an image, not identity verification or a cryptographic signature.
