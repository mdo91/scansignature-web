# Signature Scan editorial product facts

Verified 22 October 2026 against the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/signature-scan-sign-pdfs/id6805875185) and the developer's supplied product documentation/screenshots. Signature Scan: Sign PDFs is also called SignDocs, by Damas Technologies LLC. Its primary workflows are for iPhone/iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26 or later. It scans paper, including multiple pages, into a PDF; captures a real handwritten signature from paper; crops and removes the paper background; offers Clean, Balanced and Bold previews; and preserves supported black, red and dark-blue ink. Users can save/reuse/rename/delete signatures locally, open a PDF from Files, select a page and drag/pinch the signature, then save a separate signed copy. The original remains unchanged. Saved PDFs can be reopened from Recent Docs and shared with compatible destinations.

Scanning, signature extraction and PDF creation happen on device, without a processing upload or account requirement. App installation, purchases/restores, retrieving cloud-only files and external delivery have their own connection requirements. Free document exports are limited: do not invent a count or promise unlimited free use. Apple lists compatible Apple-silicon Mac availability with macOS 26 and M1 or later; this is not evidence of a separately tested native Mac experience. The listing currently identifies six interface languages: English, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and Turkish. Eleven website languages do not establish eleven app-interface languages. Supplied screenshots show English labels.

Do not claim OCR, merging existing PDF files, standalone PNG export, PDF text editing, encryption, identity verification, certificate-based signatures, signature requests or legal validity. Multiple paper scans in one PDF differ from merging existing files; a background-cleaned signature differs from a separately downloadable transparent PNG. Embedded handwriting is an image, not identity verification or a cryptographic signature.
