You can add new multiline text and checkmarks to a flat PDF in SignDocs on iPhone. Open the PDF from Files, place your answer in the blank area and review its fit before saving a separate flattened copy. The printed page stays in place: this adds an answer rather than replacing existing words.

## Place an answer deliberately

1. Download a cloud-only file while connected. Open the PDF in SignDocs and confirm the document and page count.
2. Select the page and read the question beside the blank space. A printed rectangle is not necessarily an interactive field.
3. Add new text and enter the complete answer, including any necessary line breaks.
4. Move and resize the addition so it stays inside the intended space. Read the last word of every line; inspect the bottom line for overflow.
5. Add a checkmark manually if the flat form asks for a selection. Keep it inside one box, away from the neighbouring choice.
6. Review the whole page, save a separate completed PDF and reopen it from Recent Docs. Inspect the actual saved output before sharing.

## Check a narrow answer area

Imagine a club equipment form asking which items you will bring and when. Draft “Two folding tables” on the first line and “Available after lunch” on the second. Check that the second line does not overlap the next question. If the answer will not fit legibly, shorten the wording while retaining both the quantity and timing; do not cover another question to create space.

At page width, check whether the answer belongs visibly to the correct prompt. Then zoom in to read each line and check the mark against the chosen option. A readable text size and an unambiguous selection matter more than filling every available gap.

## Choose the appropriate tool

For selectable fields, use the [form-filling guide](/blog/fill-pdf-form-iphone/). On iOS 26, Apple's Preview can add a text form box when direct form filling is unavailable; its built-in tools may be sufficient. SignDocs is useful when you also want to [capture handwriting from paper](/blog/scan-handwritten-signature-iphone/) for the completed document.

These additions do not edit or delete existing page text, perform OCR or create interactive fields. Keep the original PDF: completed exports are flattened copies, not editable form fields. SignDocs requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, and free exports are limited. Processing happens on device without an account or document upload for processing. Installation, purchases/restores, cloud retrieval and external sharing have separate connection requirements.
